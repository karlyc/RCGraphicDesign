The action control. One `primary` per view — that is the magenta "Start your order" CTA.

```jsx
<Button variant="primary" size="lg" iconAfter="arrow-right">Start your order</Button>
<Button variant="outline">Upload artwork</Button>
```

Labels are uppercase and 2-4 words, verb first. `onDark` for charcoal hero bands. Never stack two primaries.
